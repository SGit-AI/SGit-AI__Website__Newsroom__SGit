# Epic: an industry news feed in, slide decks and PDFs out

In: a daily feed of industry news on the topics of the vaults (EU AI Act, OWASP, risk and GRC, security
incidents), collected by an agent with other LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity) into JSON the newsroom consumes.
Out: daily or thematic slide decks (JSON and markdown, rendered to HTML, exported to PDF) as debriefs
that read well on a phone and can be shared on LinkedIn, Discord, Slack or email.
