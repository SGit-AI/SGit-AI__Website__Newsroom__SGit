RiskMandate.ai's offer as a value chain

A Wardley map of what riskmandate.ai sells: four levels of one Agent Behaviour Policy, the free templates and the prompt under them, the store that takes the order, and the sgit.ai vaults the whole offer is built on. The two pages that describe the paid levels do not agree on how far along they are, and the map says so.

If it connects, it is on a map, and the map cites what it drew.

This map shows riskmandate.ai's offer as its own pages describe it in the snapshot of 24 September 2026 (site v1.34.8). At the top are the buyers the site addresses. Under them are the four paid levels, then the one document every level delivers, then what that document is made from, and at the bottom what riskmandate.ai reuses from the rest of the sgit network: the store, sgit.ai's encrypted vaults and read keys, and the vocabulary pinned at abp.sgit.ai.

As with every map here, a position is a claim (wardley-maps.sgit.ai↗). The rule: what the site calls a design, or "specified, never run", is genesis; what it says "exists and runs" and carries a price is product; the payment rail and storage underneath are commodity.

What riskmandate.ai reuses from sgit.ai, in one list

  • The vault format and its host: every policy "lives in an encrypted vault you hold the keys to" (how it works↗).
  • Read keys, for the free templates (llms.txt↗).
  • The store, for the order and the payment link (store llms.txt↗).
  • The vocabulary pinned at abp.sgit.ai (home↗).
  • nhi.sgit.ai's phrase "hope is not a control", quoted as the reason a prompt is not a control (how it works↗).

The reuse also runs the other way: sgit.ai's Company X-Ray plan reuses riskmandate.ai's pricing pattern (v0.6.8, version log).
