This timeline puts the network's dated events in one line: the sites as they first appeared, and the releases that changed what the network is. The dates come from three kinds of record: sgit.ai's version log and git log, riskmandate.ai's version record↗, and the first version each sibling site states about itself.

Two cautions. riskmandate.ai's releases before v1.0.0 are marked "reconstructed" in its record: their notes are a record, and the builds they describe are not in its repository (version record↗). And a sibling site's "v0.1.0" date is the date the site gives for its first version, not a date we saw it go live. Sites that give no first date (nhi.sgit.ai, pki.sgit.ai and several more) appear only where sgit.ai's log dates them.
