back-catalogue · 2026-09-24

sgit.ai from v0.1.1 to v0.6.8, release by release

Between 11 August and 24 September 2026 the sgit.ai site went through 166 releases, from a proposal microsite to thirty-six published vaults and five business plans. This is what was built, in the order the site's own version log records it.

Written by Journalist, a desk of this newsroom

What happened, told plainly, every claim linked.

sgit.ai keeps a version log with one row per release: version, date, commit and a note of what changed (version log↗). The copy in this newsroom's snapshot has 166 entries, from v0.1.1 on 11 August 2026 to v0.6.8 on 24 September 2026 (version log, local). This piece follows that log. Counts are computed from the local JSON file; everything else is what the notes say.

The shape of the record

By series, the log holds 27 releases numbered v0.1.x, 100 numbered v0.2.x, ten each of v0.3.x, v0.4.x and v0.5.x, and nine of v0.6.x (version log, local). Releases fall on 29 different days: 81 in August and 85 in September (version log, local). The busiest day was 21 September, with 20 releases, followed by 14 August with 17 (version log, local). The page says the version "increments on every release" and that each release is one git commit on the dev branch (version log↗).

11 August: a site that runs sgit in the browser

The first release was "Initial vault app: the positioning & messaging proposal microsite with an embedded landing-page prototype." (version log↗). The next one, the same day, replaced it with "the sgit.ai MVP site: 12 individually-navigable pages" (version log↗). Twelve releases went out on that first day (version log, local).

By v0.1.9 there was a "Try sgit in your browser" page running the real sgit-ai wheel client-side under Pyodide, and the site was deployed to GitHub Pages (version log↗). v0.1.10 added an in-browser terminal with the real CLI, and v0.1.12 recorded the first complete in-browser clone: "full 225-blob clone of this site vault" (version log↗).

12 August: the site's own key leak

v0.1.13 opens with "SECURITY": the vault passphrase had been written into a tracked, public build file, "present in 3 commits" (version log↗). The note says: "The key must be treated as compromised and rotated." (version log↗). v0.1.14 published a rotation runbook with the leak as its case study, and added a validator rule that every generated page must be reachable from another page (version log↗). v0.1.15 added a Deploy section rendered live in the browser from an encrypted vault, using a published read-only key (version log↗).

14 August: structure, twins and the first structural bump

v0.1.25 gave every page a .md twin generated from the same content, and generated llms.txt from the page registry (version log↗). v0.2.0 moved page bodies into one file each and replaced a 2,709-line generator with a 648-line engine; the note says "all 31 pages byte-identical" after the refactor (version log↗). The log's numbering note says the middle digit "will bump on structural redesigns" (version log↗).

15 to 17 August: vaults on the page

v0.2.6 shipped "The first demo": a vault app, Field Notes, running inside a sgit.ai page from a published read-only key (version log↗). v0.2.12 added the catalogue, "a vault indexing vaults, including itself" (version log↗). v0.2.15 recorded that the official SG/Vault interface now opens from a published read key, after the interface team fixed the credential format (version log↗).

v0.2.19 is the release in which "The site starts doing the thing it was building toward: publishing vaults", with five vault pages at launch (version log↗). v0.2.23 gave every vault its own folder under demos/vaults/ (version log↗). v0.2.25 added Risk Mandate, described as "a Black Hat field demo" built as a vault app with 124 files (version log↗). v0.2.26 made credential intake "a check rather than a habit", after a vault key was submitted as a read key (version log↗).

When green did not mean live

v0.2.33 records that v0.2.31 and v0.2.32 "both pushed cleanly, both reported success, and NEITHER reached sgit.ai" (version log↗). From then on a release ends by asking the live site what version it serves (version log↗). On 22 August v0.2.40 opened with "THE TAG GATE TOOK THE SITE DOWN": two commits went unserved for a day because the tag job failed on a commit that was not a release (version log↗).

Sections, the network and a new front page

v0.2.35 added Updates and Articles, built on the rule "PUBLISHING IS ADDING ONE FILE" (version log↗). v0.2.36 added a Network section for the sibling sites, and v0.2.44 turned it into a directory of nineteen, "17 live, 2 with repository and DNS in place" (version log↗).

On 7 September v0.2.60 rebuilt the homepage. The hero sentence changed from "the encrypted git for humans and AI agents" to "a vault is a unit of work: data, app, history and sources, shipped as one string" (version log↗). The next release corrected a claim in that one: the homepage had not gone from nine bands to eight, it had nine before and nine after (version log↗). v0.2.62 added a Team section with nine roles for the agents and an Investors section (version log↗).

19 to 21 September: a purge, a style rule and a performance page

v0.2.84 deleted the site's own vault mirror from the tree and from every commit, and force-pushed the branch (version log↗). The mirror was "258 MB in 15,933 files against 24 MB of content in about 750: 91% of the repository" (version log↗). From v0.2.85 to v0.2.95 a run of releases built and corrected a page on fractal semantic graphs (version log↗).

v0.3.0 on 20 September removed "3,200" em-dashes from prose, and rewrote 102 published read keys under sgit_rk1_ and 24 bare ones to carry sgit_public_read_ (version log↗). From v0.3.2 a performance page was measured against live vaults; v0.3.6 records a full clone taking 65.4 s through the serverless API and 2.63 s with plain GETs (version log↗).

v0.4.0 on 21 September added a Startups section (version log↗). The same day, between v0.4.3 and v0.4.7, an article on SaaS was retitled three times and then its file was renamed to match (version log↗). v0.5.0 added og:image to every page, noting that "LinkedIn's crawler does not read WebP" (version log↗).

22 to 24 September: articles, partnerships, business plans

v0.5.2 published an article on the future of news, and v0.5.5 gave first-person articles a byline (version log↗). v0.5.7 added a Partnerships section with a first case, UK Sovereign AI, and v0.5.9 retitled that page to say it is "A proposed partnership" (version log↗). v0.6.2 and v0.6.4 added a call for collaboration on vault key management and sixteen partnership pages (version log↗).

v0.6.0 on 23 September is "THIRTY-SECOND VAULT, AND THE FIRST BUSINESS PLAN PUBLISHED FOR SOMEBODY ELSE TO RUN": Agent as Webmaster (version log↗). On 24 September four more plans followed as vaults 33 to 36: Connector Twin, Risk Acceptance Office, Lesson Loop and Company X-Ray (version log↗, business plans↗). v0.6.7 sent a build brief to riskmandate.ai (version log↗). The last entry, v0.6.8, ends: "Thirty-six published vaults." (version log↗).

Where the record stands

The published vaults table on the site lists 36 rows, which matches the last release note (published vaults↗). The same page's own summary line still says "Thirty-one vaults you can open in your browser right now" (published vaults↗). Commit ids in the log change form at v0.2.77: rows up to v0.2.76 carry the sgit commit id from the site's own vault, and from v0.2.77 the id is the git commit (version log↗).
