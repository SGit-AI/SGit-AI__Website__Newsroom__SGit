# Northgate Facilities Ltd

*Copied from the company's website, "About us" page, August 2026. Northgate Facilities is an invented company.*

Northgate Facilities keeps buildings across Yorkshire and the North West clean, safe and working. Founded in Leeds in 2009, we now look after more than 200 sites for retailers, housing providers, schools, clinics, offices and hotels.

**What we do**

- **Contract cleaning**: daily, weekly and periodic cleaning, with our own trained teams.
- **Reactive maintenance**: a 24/7 helpdesk and engineers who attend within four hours for emergencies.
- **Planned maintenance**: statutory compliance and planned preventative maintenance (PPM) schedules, from fire doors to heating and ventilation.

**Why customers choose us**

- One partner for cleaning and maintenance.
- A named supervisor for every site.
- Real-time job reporting through our mobile platform.
- Quality audits on every site, every month.

**Our people.** Around 140 people, from our Leeds head office and our Manchester team.
