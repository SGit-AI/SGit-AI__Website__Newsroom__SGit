Who buys, and when

The X-ray sells at a moment: a decision is coming, and somebody wants to walk into it with a picture rather than a feeling.

BuyerThe momentUsual level
Owner or managing director of a company with 10 to 250 people"Busier than ever and making less money." A board meeting, a bank review, a bad quarter.Standard
New managing director or CEOThe first ninety days: what did I inherit?Tailored
Chair or non-executive directorBefore a board meeting, a second view on what the pack leaves out.Standard
Fractional CFO or finance directorA new client: the first week's reading done in a day.Partner licence
Accountant or business adviserYear end, when the documents are already in hand, as an add-on to the accounts.Partner licence
Investor or acquirerA first look before paying for full due diligence.Tailored
Business owner preparing to sellSeeing what a buyer will see, first.Tailored
Charity trusteesA trustee board with a treasurer and not much else.Standard, with a sector pack

Who does not buy

  • Large companies with a finance team and a BI platform. They already have the numbers joined up; they lack other things.
  • Anyone who wants the X-ray to connect to their systems. Not in this plan. A later phase could, if the demand is there.
  • Anyone who cannot share documents at all. They can buy the kit and run it themselves, with nothing leaving their own account.
