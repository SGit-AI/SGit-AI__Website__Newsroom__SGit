# The service: four levels and a repeat

Each level is the level below plus exactly one thing, and the one thing is what the price is for. This is RiskMandate.ai's pricing rule, reused as it stands.

| Level | Price | What it is | Who does the work | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. The sample** | Free | This vault: an invented company X-rayed end to end, the catalogue and the prompts. | Nobody; read it. | You have read it. |
| **1. The kit** | £50 | The catalogue, the run prompt adapted for self-service, the intake checklist and the Claude setup, as files. | You, in your own Claude or ChatGPT. | The download's hash matches the published hash. |
| **2. Your X-ray vault** | £150 | The kit as a vault set up for your company, with your intake page, ready for you to run. Versioned, and shareable with a read-only key. | You. We set it up. | You have opened it with your key. |
| **3. Standard X-ray** | £500 | We run the catalogue on your documents, a person reviews every finding, and a thirty-minute call hands it over. Five working days. | Our agents; a person reviews. | The X-ray and the review note are committed to your vault. |
| **4. Tailored X-ray** | £1,500 | Two one-hour sessions, up to three analyses written for your questions, and a sign-off with a reviewer's name and the date. Seven working days. | A person, with our agents. | Both sessions held, and `SIGN-OFF.md` committed. |
| **Re-X-ray** | £350 or £900 | New documents in the same vault; the X-ray again, with what moved since last time. | As the level repeated. | The new X-ray is committed, with the differences listed. |

Levels 1 and 2 are the vault without customisation. Levels 3 and 4 are the vault with it.

## What the customer never gives us

- **Access to their systems.** No logins, connectors or integrations.
- **Passwords, keys or tokens.** Not at any level.
- **Documents they have not chosen.** The inbox holds only what they put there.

## What arrives, at every paid level

The page after payment says what happens now, when, and who to write to if it does not. The key arrives separately from the instructions, never on a page. Levels 2 to 4 get a reply from a person within one working day.

## The partner licence

Accountants, fractional finance directors and business advisers run X-rays for their own clients, under their own name, with the catalogue and the prompts: £250 a month plus £100 per X-ray, with the review done by the partner. The catalogue improves from every partner's X-rays, with the partner's permission and without the client's documents.
