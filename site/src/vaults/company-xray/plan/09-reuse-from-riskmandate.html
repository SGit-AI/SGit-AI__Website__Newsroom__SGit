What is reused from RiskMandate.ai

The X-ray is a different product with the same shape. Most of what RiskMandate.ai has already built and published carries over as it stands.

RiskMandate.ai hasThe X-ray reuses it as
Four levels: the pack for £10, a working vault for £50, corrected for £500, two sessions and a professional's signature for £1,500.Four levels: the kit, the vault, Standard and Tailored, at £50, £150, £500 and £1,500.
"Each level is the level below plus exactly one thing."The same rule, stated on the pricing page.
Free published examples with read keys, before anybody buys.The sample X-ray in this vault.
The prompt is free on purpose.The run prompt and the catalogue are public.
Standing payment links at store.sgit.ai, and a page after payment that says what happens next.The same, one link per level.
"Done is a commit", checkable from the vault's own history.Each level's "done when" is a commit in the customer's vault.
Reviewer pages: the people who sign, and what is published about them.The reviewers who sign Tailored X-rays.
Labels for how a row is known: measured, documented or derived.Read, computed or inferred.
"What we never ask for": credentials, access, scans.The same list, on the intake page.

What is different

  • The subject. RiskMandate describes one AI agent in one deployment. The X-ray describes a whole company, from its documents.
  • The input. RiskMandate's prompt runs where the agent runs. The X-ray's documents are dropped into a vault.
  • The buyer. RiskMandate sells to the people who answer for an agent. The X-ray sells to the people who answer for the company.

Together

A founder could run both from one store, with one reviewer pool and one pattern for delivery. That is a decision for the founders, not for this plan.
