Who adds what

The founder's own question: how much value do we add, and how much does the customer provide? The answer shapes the price, the pitch and what to protect.

WhoAddsWorth
The customerThe documents, the context, and the questions. Nobody else has them.Most of the value. Without the documents there is nothing to X-ray.
The modelReading everything, doing the arithmetic at speed, and noticing where documents disagree.Makes the service possible at this price. Available to everybody.
The operatorThe catalogue, meaning which questions to ask of which documents. The discipline of evidence and labels. The review. The packaging, meaning the vault, the board pack and the Claude setup.What turns a chat into something a board can use and a customer can check.

What follows from that

  • Price low, because the customer does half the work. £500 is a fee for questions, discipline and a review. It is not a fee for knowing the customer's business better than they do.
  • Give the method away. The sample, the catalogue and the prompts are public. A customer who runs the kit themselves is a customer who has learnt why the review matters.
  • Protect the review and the catalogue's improvement. Those are what a competitor with the same model cannot copy in a week.
  • Say it plainly in the pitch: "It is your data and your questions. We bring the right questions to ask of it, check every answer, and leave you set up to keep asking."
