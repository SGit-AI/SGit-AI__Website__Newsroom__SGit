Mock-ups

Four standalone pages, each a single HTML file with its styles inlined and no external assets, so they open anywhere, including from this vault. The businesses are invented.

FileWhat it shows
customer-bakery.htmlA neighbourhood bakery. Hours, the week's bread, a notice, a gallery. The kind of site that changes every Friday.
customer-accountants.htmlA two-partner accountancy firm. Services, the team, deadlines, a contact form placeholder. Credibility, kept current.
customer-physio.htmlA physiotherapy clinic. Treatments, the team, a booking embed placeholder, prices.
sales-site.htmlThe operator's own site: what the service is, the three mock-ups as examples, the prices published, and how to start.

Each customer mock-up carries a "changed by the webmaster" strip near the top, showing the last three changes and when they went live. That strip is the product: a website that visibly gets updated.

To turn a mock-up into a real customer site, follow the setup.
