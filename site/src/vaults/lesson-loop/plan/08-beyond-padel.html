8. Beyond padel

The loop works wherever one person teaches another in sessions, and knows the most about the student at the end of each one.

DomainThe moment to captureWhat the student adds between sessions
Tennis, squash, pickleballThe end of the lessonMatch notes and clips
GolfThe end of the lesson, on the rangeRounds played, scorecards, swing clips
Music lessonsThe end of the lessonPractice logs and recordings
Language tutoringThe end of the sessionReal conversations, words that failed them
Personal training, physiotherapyThe end of the sessionWorkouts done, pain and progress
Driving instructionThe end of the lessonPractice drives with a family member
Climbing, swimmingThe end of the sessionRoutes or sessions done alone

The pieces do not change: a voice memo, an agent, a record the student holds, a briefing for the next session. Each domain needs its own note template and its own themes.
