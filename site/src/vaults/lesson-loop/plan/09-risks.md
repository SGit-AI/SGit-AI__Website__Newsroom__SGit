# 9. Risks

| Risk | Why it matters | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Children** | Many students are minors. Recording, notes and clips about children need parental consent and a safeguarding policy. | Adults only in the first phases. Design consent and a guardian's key before any junior programme. |
| **Other people in clips** | Match clips show opponents and partners who did not agree to be recorded. | Clips for the coach only, not shared further; blur or crop where possible; ask partners. |
| **Personal data** | A player's record is personal data, and coaches' notes can be candid. | The player holds the key, and can delete the vault. Notes are about play, not the person. |
| **Clubs feel bypassed** | Direct coach-player relationships can look like leakage. | Share remote revenue with clubs, and sell through them. |
| **The agent gets it wrong** | A note that misstates what the coach said is worse than none. | The transcript is kept beside every note, and the coach can correct it. |
| **Coaches do not keep it up** | Two minutes at the end of every lesson is a habit to form. | Start with coaches who already care; measure it; make it the last drill of the lesson. |
| **Key management** | Players lose keys; coaches leave. | Few keys per player; lean on password managers; the call for collaboration on key management. |
| **Undisclosed commissions** | Kit recommendations that pay the coach damage trust. | Every recommendation states any commercial interest. |
