newsroom.sgit.ai — Brief Pack

Start with 00__BRIEF.md. Then read 02__source-provenance-and-attribution.md before republishing anything.

A brief pack for building newsroom.sgit.ai — The Future of News — a new sibling on the sgit.ai network covering graphs, provenance, trust, content rights and micropayments in journalism.

Prepared 21 August 2026 against the-cyber-boardroom/SGraph-AI__App__Send @ v0.33.62, DinisCruz/docs.diniscruz.ai @ v0.3.123, and DinisCruz/files.diniscruz.ai.

Tiers

  • Tier 0 (28) — the site's own material, publishable near-as-is. 18 from __Send, 10 published articles
  • Tier 1 (13) — publishable after de-scoping or a licence check
  • Tier 2 (3) — cite, don't republish; belongs to a sibling site
  • Tier 3 (4) — do not publish (live company detail, named VC, source-protection safety)

Verification

All 35 __Send paths checked against v0.33.62. All 13 docs.diniscruz.ai URLs resolved live on 21 August 2026, along with their PDFs and LinkedIn posts. Numbers from published vaults are cited to the live page; numbers from repo documents are cited to the document. The pack never mixes the two.

The one-line summary

~110,000 words across three corpora, of which 68,846 are already public and dated — so this site launches as the continuation of a two-year, publicly-checkable thread, not as a manifesto. The provenance chain back to those originals is the site's first demonstration of its own thesis.
