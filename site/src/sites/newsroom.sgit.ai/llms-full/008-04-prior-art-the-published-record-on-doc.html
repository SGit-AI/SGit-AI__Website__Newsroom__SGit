04 — Prior Art: the published record on docs.diniscruz.ai

Ten core articles, 68,846 words, published February – October 2025. A year before the __Send material, in the founder's public voice, already indexed and already circulated on LinkedIn.

This is the site's strongest single asset. It means newsroom.sgit.ai does not launch as a manifesto — it launches as the continuation of a two-year, dated, publicly-checkable thread. Lead with that.

Every URL below was resolved live on 21 August 2026. Machine-readable equivalents, including repo paths and git first-commit dates, are in sources__docs-diniscruz-ai.json.

Before republishing anything here, read 02__source-provenance-and-attribution.md. The contract is: original date, original link, original co-authors, honest curation label.
