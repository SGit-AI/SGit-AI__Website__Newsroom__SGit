Mihály Csíkszentmihályi

An influence on Dinis Cruz — one of 25 entries in his register.

Named directly in the corpus with his flow criteria applied, which is why he gets a row of his own rather than a mention inside the flow entry. The state has a specification; the specification is what makes it engineerable.

Block 1 — The anchor

Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi — 1990

Shared with the flow entry↗, deliberately. That entry is about the methodology built from the research; this one is about the research.

Linked, never rehosted.

Block 2 — In his own words

The move that matters is the one people skip: Csikszentmihalyi did not describe a mood, he specified a state — clear goals, immediate feedback, challenge matched to skill, merging of action and awareness, loss of self-consciousness, altered sense of time. A list of preconditions is a thing you can design against. A vibe is not.

That is why he is here as a separate entry from the methodology built on him. The methodology is a claim about what to do; the research is the claim that the thing being protected is real and has criteria, and the second claim is what makes the first one more than a preference.

He also, usefully, named programming as one of the activities that induces the state — which the corpus cites directly rather than assuming.

Block 3 — The principle

Optimal experience has measurable preconditions, which means environments can be engineered for it rather than hoped for.

Block 4 — The trace table

No trace table yet. The trace of this influence is the flow entry's table ↗, and duplicating it here would be the same mistake the deconfliction rule prevents between sites. This entry owns the research; that one owns the methodology and the rows.

Block 6 — The checklist

  • Are all three preconditions present — clear goal, immediate feedback, challenge matched to skill — or only the ones that were easy to build?
  • Is the state being designed for, or hoped for?
  • Which of the six criteria could actually be observed here, and is anyone observing them?

Block 7 — The wider library

  • Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience (1990) — the anchor. Chapter three is where the criteria are set out
  • Beyond Boredom and Anxiety (1975) — the earlier empirical work, with the experience-sampling method that produced the data
  • Creativity (1996) — the same method applied to people doing original work over decades
