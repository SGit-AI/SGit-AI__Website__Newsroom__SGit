WHAT IS NOT IN THIS FILE, BY RULE: the anchor works. This site explains why an influence resonated and traces where it was applied; it does not reproduce the talks, essays, books or lyrics that did the influencing. Those are linked, never rehosted — they belong to their authors and a CC BY file that carried them would be relicensing what this site does not own. Short attributed quotation for analysis is capped at 40 words by the release gate. Follow the anchor URLs for the real thing.
