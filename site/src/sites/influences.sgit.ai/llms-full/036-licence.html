What CC BY explicitly does NOT cover

The anchor works. Bret Victor's talks and transcripts, Simon Wardley's book, Kevin Kelly's books, Rush's music and lyrics, David Rice's keynote, Csikszentmihalyi's Flow, A Pattern Language, The Cathedral and the Bazaar, and every other cited work belong to their authors and estates. The site's rule (set by the founder, and correct in law): link, never rehost; quote only short passages under analysis; lyrics near-zero; likenesses only as original artwork. See 04__boundaries-and-licensing.md — the no-verbatim rule is what lets the CC BY stamp on the site's own text stay honest.

An agent ingesting /llms-full.txt inherits the same split: the file must never contain a third party's expression beyond attributed analytical quotation.
