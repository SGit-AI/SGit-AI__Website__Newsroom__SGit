Music, and playing in a band

The most personal entry on the site, entirely awaiting Dinis Cruz's account — and carrying the hypothesis that, if it holds, explains one of the most-traced influences in the register.

Block 1 — The anchor

Playing in a band — a personal era

Not a work, and not a public event. There is nothing to link, which makes this the only entry on the site with no anchor URL at all.

Linked, never rehosted.

Block 2 — In his own words

The hypothesis is worth stating precisely because of what depends on it. The flow entry ↗ is one of the best-traced things in this register: Csikszentmihalyi's state, Victor's mechanism, and a development methodology whose stated core principle is preserving flow. If band-playing is where Dinis Cruz first experienced that state, then the most-instrumented influence on this site has an origin that is not written down anywhere.

It also has a specific property none of the software influences do: a band is collaborative flow. The methodology tries to recreate a state between a person and a machine. The original may have been a state between people — and that difference, if real, is a design constraint nobody has written down.

Block 3 — The principle

(hypothesis) Playing together in real time is the original experience of collaborative flow — several people, immediate feedback, no possibility of stopping to plan.

This is the site's most consequential unverified claim and it is flagged as such everywhere it appears. If Dinis Cruz confirms it, it becomes the root of a lineage. If he does not, it is a neat story invented by a machine, and the register will say so.

Awaiting the briefing document

Entirely unresearchable by anyone except Dinis Cruz. There is no public record, no publication to mine and no third party to ask. This entry is a placeholder for something only he can write, and it is published so that its absence is visible rather than tidy.

Research plan. There is no research plan, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. The entry needs N6 ↗ and nothing else. The specific questions worth answering: what the band was and when; whether the flow hypothesis above is right or a machine's tidy story; and whether the collaborative aspect is the part that carried over.

The corpus evidence

None. This influence is on Dinis Cruz's own list and the corpus scan found nothing to attach to it — which is the definition of the STATED tier.
