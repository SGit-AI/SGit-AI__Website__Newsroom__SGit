v0.10.1: the desktop walkthrough gets its article, with six figures captured from the v0.10.0 tag

One article per release, so the release that added the desktop walkthrough gets one. It covers why the third walkthrough keeps the shape of the first two, why the switch is the character of the desktop shape, why the map of what matters comes before the rules and why every rule names the group of the map it follows from, and why a toggle is a setting on your own machine and a boundary on a managed one. Six screenshots from a checkout of the v0.10.0 tag on the day it shipped.
