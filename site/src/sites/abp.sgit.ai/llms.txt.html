abp.sgit.ai

The Agent Behaviour Policy: what your agent can do, what you authorised it to do, the gap between them, and what actually stands in the way.

Site version: v0.11.0

Every page below has a markdown twin generated from the same content as the HTML page. Fetch the .md and you have the page, without the chrome. The whole site in one file is at /llms-full.txt.

This site publishes the record and never the verdict. There is no score, no rating and no risk level anywhere on it, including in the data.
