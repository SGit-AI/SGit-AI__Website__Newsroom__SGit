Startup Summit Lisbon 2026 — RiskMandate materials

Everything Startup Summit needs from RiskMandate in one place: the name, the description at four lengths, every version of the logo, and the booth panel artwork.

Source: https://riskmandate.ai/summit-booth.html↗

RiskMandate, in one page.

Everything Startup Summit needs from us: how the name is written, the description at four lengths, every version of the logo, and the booth panel artwork. Take what you need from here rather than waiting on an email.

The name, and how it is written.

The one thing worth getting exactly right, because it is printed and it is how somebody asks for us by name on the floor.

  • NameRiskMandate — one word, two capitals, no space and no hyphen
  • Websiteriskmandate.ai↗
  • What we do, in four wordsAgent Behaviour Policies
  • CategoryCybersecurity
  • Dates17–18 September 2026, Beato Innovation District

The description, at four lengths.

Four versions of the same thing, so there is one that fits whatever field it is going into. They say the same thing in the same order, so choosing a shorter one loses detail and never changes the claim. Use the longest that fits.

None of these names a customer, a funder or an insurer, and none attaches an adjective to anybody else’s product. British spelling throughout. The same four are on our brand page↗ if you would rather link than paste.

The lockup, and the mark alone.

Two things, and the difference matters. The lockup is the mark plus the word RiskMandate, and it is what to use almost everywhere. The mark is the square seal on its own, for a place where only a square will fit.

Lockup — for light backgrounds The default. Use this one unless the background is dark. SVG ↗PNG · 2948×800 ↗

Lockup — for dark backgrounds The same lockup in the light ink, for a dark ground. SVG ↗PNG · 2948×800 ↗

Mark — square For an avatar, an app tile, or any slot that is square. SVG ↗512px ↗300px ↗128px ↗

Mark — knockout A single-colour cut, for one-colour printing or an embroidered surface. SVG ↗Simplified SVG ↗

  • Use the lockup, not the mark alone, anywhere a stranger will see it. The mark is recognisable to people who already know us. The word is what lets somebody ask for us by name.
  • SVG wherever it scales, PNG where a raster is required. Every PNG here is transparent, so it sits on whatever ground it lands on.
  • Ink and green only. Never recoloured, never stretched, never set on a busy photograph, and the detailed cut never below 24px — below that use the simplified one. The full brand page↗ has the rest.
