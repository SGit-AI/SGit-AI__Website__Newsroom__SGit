RiskMandate — thank you: level 1, what happens now

After paying for an Agent Behaviour Policy at level 1: what arrives and when, what you do next, how the key reaches you, the definition of done, and who to write to.

Source: https://riskmandate.ai/paid-t1.html↗

Thank you. Here is what happens now.

You bought an Agent Behaviour Policy at level 1: the files. Keep the payment receipt; its reference is how you quote this order. The product code is t1.

Your order reference on your payment receipt · product code t1

Download it now. The zip is here, not in an email.

Every file in the vault for the shape you picked, as one zip: the Agent Behaviour Policy, the grant, the mandate and the delta as markdown and as JSON, the pinned vocabulary, the scenarios, the prompts, and the two files you hand the agent. Its content hash is printed beside the link, so you can check that what you downloaded is what the shape publishes. Keep the address of this page: the link stays valid, and the hash stays what it is.

sha256 bytes

Unzip it, read MANDATE.md first, and hand AGENTS.md and the three documents beside it to the agent. README.md says what each file is.

Which shape did you buy? The receipt names it. Pick it below and the download appears; every template is the same price, so picking is all that is needed.

Check the hash. Then read the mandate.

Compare the hash printed above with the hash of what you downloaded, or press check the hash here and the page does it in your browser. Then unzip it: MANDATE.md is what the agent was authorised to do, GRANT.md is everything it can reach, DELTA.md is the gap between them, with the barrier on every row. If the gap is wider than you thought, that is the finding, and the next level is where it gets corrected for your agent rather than the template's.

Done is a commit. You can check it.

This level is done when: The zip for the shape you bought is downloadable from this page and its content hash matches the hash the shape publishes.

Checked by: Compare the hash printed beside the link with the hash of what you downloaded, here in the browser or with sha256sum. You do not have to take anybody's word for it.

Write to a person. Not a form.

If the download fails, the hash does not match, or the shape on your receipt is not on this page, email nrparekh@gmail.com with your order reference and the product code t1. A person reads it and sends the file.
