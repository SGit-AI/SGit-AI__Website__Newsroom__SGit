How work gets here

The memo queue →↗ — a document or a memo from the project lead arrives, is archived byte for byte and registered by digest, and is read into a brief. What was said and what we made of it are two objects, and only one of them is allowed to be wrong.

The briefs →↗ — what was decided and why, written here against a named source, one page each. A brief is broken into task briefs sized for one agent.

The board →↗ — the queue, in four columns. A row moves because its status in the state file moved or because a branch claimed its brief; nothing is dragged.
