Accepted is not acceptable · talks and the proposition deck · RiskGraph

The first tranche of the brief-corpus proposals, plus the public artefacts.

  • New page: acceptable.html. The distinction the site had never carried — accepted is an act somebody performs; acceptable is the level at which the business stops funding remediation. The centrepiece is an interactive four-state grid (they are orthogonal, not sequential), because missing acceptance is an ownership problem and a missing acceptable line is a governance problem — different failures, different fixes. Also on the page: acceptable as risk appetite renamed, the Article 9(5) hook (the Act mandates the judgement without ever defining the word), declared vs revealed appetite as the probe that needs no tooling, the full interval ladder with the response each rung commits you to, and accept-first with the risk level as a dated ledger.
  • Talks and the proposition deck are now on the Library page. The 12-slide value-proposition PDF is embedded, read through the vault bridge at runtime like the rest of the library content — no bytes inlined into the page. Talks are click-to-load: nothing is fetched from YouTube until the visitor presses play, and the player is youtube-nocookie. Both are driven by assets/media/media.json, so adding a talk is a content edit with no rebuild.
  • Plug page extended — speed restated as a race (time-to-pull against time-to-damage) with the detection floor now grounded and attributed: hyperscaler spend is typically reported 12–18 hours in arrears, so a cost-triggered response cannot start sooner. Plus the precise-plug vs blunt-plug distinction (the absence of a precise plug is itself a finding) and the altitude point (recoverability worsens as you climb). Cross-links to the new page in the FAQ.
  • Two library articles: Recoverability and Accepted is not acceptable. Article artwork is now optional, so writing is no longer blocked on an infographic.
  • Naming: RiskGraph. SGraph is gone from the site; the architecture diagram now reads RISKGRAPH. Historical frozen versions keep their own wording, as the immutability model requires.
  • Housekeeping: removed the src/versions old-structure duplicate (resurrected by a merge again), and the smoke suite's library and page counts are now derived from content rather than hardcoded.
