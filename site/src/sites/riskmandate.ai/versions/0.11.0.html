New homepage: the grant is not the mandate · live vault demos · RiskMandate

The A/B release: a new presentation of the same operating model. v0.11.0 is the partner-designed homepage, ported and reframed; v0.10.0 — the acceptance-led presentation — stays selectable in the switcher and reachable from the nav, so the two ways of explaining what we do can be compared on the live site.

  • The homepage now leads with the gap. A grant is what the credential technically permits; a mandate is what the holder is authorised and expected to do; excess authority — grant minus mandate — is blast radius read from the other end: held, unauthorised, and *unaccepted by construction*, so it defaults to critical and escalates without anybody escalating it. The caveat is stated on the page, plainly: **we define the mandate and map the gap; we do not enforce it** — a declared mandate is instrumentation, not a control, and you instrument before you enforce. (Per the v0.33.61 brief, which corrects the 17 July grant-equals-mandate claim; the concept-map review carries the correction too.)
  • The design keeps the partner's structure — mandate card hero, no-and-go failure chain, four-stage lifecycle with the struck-through third door, layer map, bento, comparison, principles, FAQ — restyled from the neon palette to the site's ink/green and system fonts, with the enforcement copy reframed for never-in-the-request-path throughout (ENFORCE & OBSERVE → OBSERVE; REVOKE → EXPIRE: the acceptance runs out and lands back on the same desk).
  • New top-level page: Live demos. Three published vaults embedded natively, the same technique sgit.ai uses: a sandboxed iframe of the SG/Vault host with the deliberately published read-only key in the URL fragment — which the browser never sends to a server. Click-to-load, so the page makes no external requests until asked. RiskGraph Explorer (seven views; no risk orphaned; nobody accepts on anybody else's behalf), Agentic Browser Isolation (a page per stakeholder altitude; acceptance-gated escalation; no deny door), and the RiskMandate field demo (eight questions to a register; uses an LLM without ever holding the API key).
  • The previous homepage is preserved twice: selectable as v0.10.0 in the switcher, and linked from the nav as You own the risk — a manifest-level entry pointing at the existing v0.10.0 output, duplicating nothing.
  • The brand is RiskMandate, one word, across every page, the host, and the llms/agent artifacts; a smoke assertion guards it. Frozen versions keep their historical wording. Demo wiring stays on the interim address until hello@riskmandate.ai exists.
  • Smoke grows to 18 groups, including guards that the homepage carries the gap thesis and never reacquires the enforcement language or the neon palette.
