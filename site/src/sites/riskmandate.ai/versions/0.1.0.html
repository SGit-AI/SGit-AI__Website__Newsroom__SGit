Baseline

First versioned snapshot of the Risk Mandate marketing site, delivered as an encrypted SG/Vault HTML app.

  • Hero "Agents act. You own the risk." with an interactive risk queue: accept or request info on each agent, with live counts and a derived risk score.
  • Sections: the category framing, the Lethal Trifecta, the Four Pillars (map · accept · fund · reduce), Hope vs. Enforce risk acceptance, Capability Certificates, and pricing.
  • Renders in three contexts from one file: the SG/App host, the vault preview, and a plain static domain.
