The ladder has steps

The lead asked for the word rung to go when the deck was rebuilt for November: it is not a common word, and it reads oddly, most of all to somebody reading in a second language. The deck dropped it then and How it works↗ never had it. It was still on eleven pages.

  • Every page now says step. Describe it, licence it, insure it are steps one, two and three, and the first step is what is on sale. The eyebrows on Licence to Operate↗ and Insurance↗, the three cards on Insure a program↗, the heading on Try it↗, and the sentence in the article↗ that says which one we are on.

  • Where step would have been wrong, a different word. On Acceptable↗ the word was naming an interval, so it now says interval. On Brand↗, create or buy is a choice between two ways in, not two steps. On the ABP page↗, the prescription is the only one of the three with a score on it.

  • The class names went too. Nobody reads a class name, but the next person to search the repository for the word would, and would find it on every vault page. The print rules, the Index ladder's buttons and the vault stylesheet now say tier or level.

  • A test keeps it gone. Any page, markdown twin or llms.txt that says rung fails the build, with the sentence in the message. The rule is also in the notes every agent reads first.

  • The Index ladder on Insurance↗ has its colours back. Checking the renamed buttons showed six empty boxes with pale text. The six level colours were defined on the old home page, and when the ladder moved to Insurance they stayed behind. They are now on the page that draws the ladder. This was broken before today, not by the rename.

What still says it, on purpose: the release notes, the brief register and a registered brief. They are records of what was written on their date, and those are never rewritten.
