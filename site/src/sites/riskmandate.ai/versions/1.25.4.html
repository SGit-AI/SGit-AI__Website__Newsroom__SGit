One menu on the home page, and Lisbon comes out of the nav

The home page had two navigation bars. The design the partner sent carried its own in-page anchor strip — Why now · How it works · For leaders · Pricing — with a buy button on the right, and v1.25.1↗ kept it under the shared menu on the grounds that in-page anchors add to a site menu rather than replace it. On the page that is two menus, and since v1.25.3↗ relabelled the buttons it was also the buy action twice above the fold. The strip and its CSS are gone. Buy an Agent Behaviour Policy now appears once in the hero and once in the closing panel, which is once at each end of a page seven screens long. The section ids stay, so the link from the brief register↗ to index.html#parts still lands where it did.

Lisbon 2026 is out of the menu. The Startup Summit was 17–18 September 2026 and today is the second day, so from here it is a dated record rather than a destination. The page↗ is untouched: unlisted takes a page out of the menu data and does nothing else, so it keeps its URL, its markdown twin, its entry in sitemap.xml and llms.txt, and stays indexable. The materials page↗ it links was already unlisted, for the same reason. The header is down to six top-level entries.

And a proposal for the rest of the menu, which is the lead's call rather than ours: the menu after the ABP turn↗. The short version: the menu still describes a site that argued for insurability first and started selling behaviour policies later. Eleven listed pages have no editorial link from anywhere but the menu; two entries four rows apart are called Agent Behaviour Policy and *Agent Behaviour Policies*; and the Insurance group carries eight pages in front of a reader who came to buy one thing. The proposal is five top-level entries with the product first, nineteen pages in the menu rather than thirty-four, and nothing deleted or hidden — with two alternatives written down beside it, including the four-entry shape the partner's design implies.
