## Pricing

Three tiers, split by value. Every tier runs the same product on the same zero-knowledge foundation; what changes per tier is how it is operated — its maintainability, scalability, and security posture — not which features are gated. Sovereignty is the floor, not the premium.

### Community — Free (open source)

- **Maintainability:** Open source, files and folders, no database to operate — no lock-in.
- **Scalability:** Adopt incrementally, as much or as little as fits your stack, at your pace.
- **Security:** Zero-knowledge vault on your own infrastructure — your keys, your data.

### Consumption — Token-based (metered by usage)

- **Maintainability:** Managed runtime — versioned, provenance on every change, agent-operable.
- **Scalability:** Grows with usage; pre-approval against risk profiles keeps acceptance scaling without nagging.
- **Security:** Same zero-knowledge foundation — plaintext never leaves your endpoints.

### Enterprise — Custom (talk to us)

- **Maintainability:** SLAs, support, and guided upgrades across the version chain.
- **Scalability:** The underwriting chain org-wide — acceptance propagated level by level to a board view.
- **Security:** Sovereign deployment: your region, your keys; NIST / ISO / EU AI Act alignment.
