How long will you accept this risk?

Not whether. Four situations you'd refuse on instinct — each already accepted the moment an agent runs. The only real question is the interval.

You'll say no. Then check the record.

Each card asks a question with an obvious answer, offers you a way out, and then closes it. Your decisions are stamped into the card as you go — that's the point.

Whether is a dodge. How long is a commitment.

The risk already exists

For a deployed agent, the grant has been made and the authority is live. "No" describes a world that has already ended; acceptance just makes the implicit explicit.

No deny — only accept

A real risk can't be denied, only accepted: in a direction, by an owner, and for an interval. That's the mechanic these cards rehearse on your own instincts.

The interval is the decision

An hour means revoke now. Six months means an explicit, visible, expiring deferral. Naming the interval is what turns a worry into an owned decision.

You just did the product.

Every card ended the same way: an owned, time-boxed acceptance with a record behind it. That's the whole mechanic — mapped to your agents, underwritten to your board.
