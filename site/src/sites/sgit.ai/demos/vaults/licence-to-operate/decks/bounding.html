What this deck is doing

The problem it starts fromThe delta is enormous and I cannot close all of it
What it answersBound the parts that matter: bands, limits, refusals
Vocabulary it introducesthe band · the limit · the refusal
Who it is forsomebody who has seen the delta, tried to close it, and found that some of it is load-bearing

Where it deliberately stopspools, draws, acceptors, budgets, price, a bound needs none of them
