Two ways to build a company on it

On your own. Instead of join and work on my thing: build your thing on top of what is already built. The code is permissive and the documents are CC BY.

With me, on one of three rungs:

  • One · Direct hire. Cash per mini project. Lowest commitment, both ways.
  • Two · Revenue share. Time-boxed, no entity. Voice Debrief runs this way: 90 days; a quarter on net receipts; an even split of what remains. Either party may continue or fork on exit.
  • Three · A company. Shares and equity. Highest commitment, both ways.

Everything is open, so there is almost nothing to protect: the agreement collapses to the split, the term, and a short list of sensitive items.
