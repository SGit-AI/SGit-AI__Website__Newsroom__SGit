Corporate users, Services available now

How do I manage, contain, monitor and ensure compliance of the agents already running? These are services you can engage now, some are live products, some are designs delivered as engagements.

Each row states what it is, what it runs on, and how far along it is.

Download the PDF ↓ ↗The sheet as handed out in Lisbon. The table below is the same content, in a form a search engine and an agent can read.
