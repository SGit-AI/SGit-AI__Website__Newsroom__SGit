The person behind the first-person articles

Dinis Cruz

sgit.ai is built by one person and a team of agents, and the articles here that say I are written by the person. This page is the provenance for that byline: who I am, what I have built, where else I write, and the interests a reader should know about before taking the argument. It follows the same rule as the rest of the site, which is that a claim should be checkable without trusting me.

The record

RoleWhat it involves
Founder, sgit.ai↗Encrypted vaults with git semantics: clone, commit, branch and merge files that are encrypted before they leave your machine, under Apache-2.0, and the network of sites↗ of which this is the hub. Each site publishes its argument before its implementation, so the commitments are checkable, and thirty-six vaults are published with their read keys↗ as the evidence.
Founder, sgraph.ai ↗Where the strategy turns into revenue: the commercial home of SG/Send, the secure file-sharing service built on the open-source sgit layer, and hosted SG/Vaults. The code stays Apache-2.0. What is sold is the running, maintained, certified service.
Founder, MyFeeds.ai ↗Role-aware cybersecurity briefings built on semantic knowledge graphs, with source attribution: CISO, engineer and board views of the same news. Open source, serverless, and with the seed pitch and unit economics published in the open.

Apache-2.0, with the community edition and the investment repository ↗ public. Founder, VoiceDebrief.ai ↗Voice recordings into transcripts and debriefs, entirely in the browser, with nothing uploaded to a server. The vault where Fractal Semantic Graphs were first worked↗ came out of this work.

Interests declared

I run companies whose strategy the articles here describe, and which sell the running, maintained service rather than the code. The market the news article, the SaaS article and the startups section describe is one I intend to be in. Read the arguments knowing that. Two things are built in to keep it honest.

  • The claims are checkable without trusting me. The measurements are printed with the commands that produced them↗, the vaults are open with a read key↗, the method is written down with the mistakes that produced each rule↗, and the lessons learned↗ include the ones that cut against the argument.
  • The pitch is public too. sgit.ai for investors↗ is published in the open, with the ask left visibly empty until it is stated rather than invented.
