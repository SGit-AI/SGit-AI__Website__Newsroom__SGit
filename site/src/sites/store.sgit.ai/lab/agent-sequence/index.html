Describe your agent — one at a time

Nothing on this page can be bought. A prototype of an interface.

One family per screen, in order, with what each reach means beside it.

  • Good at: For somebody working alone, with nobody to explain the words. Each screen is small enough to answer without deciding what the whole model is first.
  • Bad at: Nine screens is nine chances to stop, and it hides how much is left. It also makes the shape of the grant hard to see until the end — which is the thing the document is actually about.
  • 23 primitives on a verb.object.reach grammar, promoted from riskmandate.ai's template vault
  • It produces the GRANT only — not the mandate, not the delta, not a policy
  • What you build stays in this browser and leaves as a file you copy out
