Gmail, read-only scope

The narrowest scope that reads one message reads every message. Lab 03 asked the model site for this shape first; here it is, read from Google's scope page.

  • It can do: 4
  • You wanted: 1
  • Not wanted: 3
  • Nothing in the way: 2

Counts, not a score.

The four levels

  • ABP-GML-P — £10 — The pack, downloaded: Every file in the vault, downloaded by you. Yours to use, keep and hand to your agent.
  • ABP-GML-V — £50 — A working vault: The same material as a vault you hold the keys to: clone it, change it, version it, and hand anyone a read key.
  • ABP-GML-C — £500 — Corrected for your situation: You tell us the industry, the use case and the details. The mandate is corrected against them and the vault comes back yours.
  • ABP-GML-S — £1,500 — Two sessions and a custom vault: Half an hour with your team to find out what is actually running, and half an hour to deliver it — reviewed and signed off by a security professional.
