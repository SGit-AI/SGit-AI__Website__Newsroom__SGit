What is actually in one

Four objects, and the order they come in matters.

  • The mandate — what the agent is authorised and expected to do. Elicited, because you already know it.
  • The grant — everything it can actually do. Measured, from the deployment shape, the account and the credentials. It is almost never enumerated anywhere, and it is reliably larger than whoever deployed it expected.
  • The delta — permitted, and nobody asked for it. Derived, and recomputed whenever either input moves.
  • The barrier — what stands between the agent and each capability. Four kinds, and only one of them is a control: a setting the agent's own account can flip is not one, because the grant includes the ability to remove it.

A grant on its own is an inventory, and nobody acts on an inventory. *Your agent can do sixteen things* is a shrug. *Your agent can do sixteen things and you authorised five* is a finding.

There is no score here, and there will not be one

**The same policy is dangerous in one deployment and harmless in another, and nothing about the document changed.** The most permissive grant imaginable, running where there is nothing to reach, is a low risk; the same grant with a production database attached tomorrow is a different one. Risk is a function of the behaviour policy, the assets, the consequences and the date — **and the behaviour policy is the input that does not move.**

So there is no rating on anything here, no traffic light and no risk level. **A policy cannot be dangerous. A deployment can.**

The free thing, and the paid thing

The templates are free and public, with published read keys, at riskmandate.ai. Go and read one before you buy anything here. That is not a generosity and it is not a funnel: a policy nobody can check is a policy asking to be trusted, and the whole point of this document type is that it can be checked.

What is priced here is an instance. The same template with the mandate corrected, your name on the licence and no public key on it — and at the upper two levels, the correction done with you rather than by you. **The paid thing is not access and it is not customisation. It is independence:** at every level more of the result is yours and less of it depends on us still being here.
