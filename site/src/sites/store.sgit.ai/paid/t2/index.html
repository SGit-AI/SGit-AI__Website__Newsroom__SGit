You bought: A working vault

£50 · 1 to 2 days, from your payment.

What happens now

A person follows up within 24 hours of the payment landing. The vault key comes by a separate message.

Nothing until the follow-up. Then clone the vault with the key.

What done looks like

A vault exists, the licence file in it carries your name, the public key is off it, and you have opened it with your key.

How you check it: Your first clone. The licence file is in the tree.
