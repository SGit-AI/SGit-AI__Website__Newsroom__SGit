Tier 1 — Can I just have the pack?

Every file in the vault for the shape you picked, downloaded by you — the behaviour policy, the grant and the mandate as markdown and as JSON, the pinned vocabulary, the prompts, the business case, and the two files you hand the agent — under a commercial licence to you, rather than under the attribution licence the same files carry in public.

  • What is true of it today: Built, and it runs today. Fifteen template shapes are published, and the pack for any of them is what this level sends.

What arrives

  • A download, on the page you land on the moment the payment goes through — the zip of the whole pack for the shape you chose, with its size and its sha256 beside it.
  • A commercial licence to you over everything in it. The same files are published free under CC BY, which obliges you to attribute them wherever they end up. This licence removes that: use the pack in client work, inside a product, in a document that goes to a regulator, without carrying our name into it.
  • The behaviour policy, the grant, the mandate, the delta and the barrier on each row.
  • The prompts and documents you hand the agent, and the business case for a control.

What this is not, and will not become

  • Not a vault. No history, no app, and no read key to hand anybody — that is the level above.
  • The licence does not make the files private. They stay public under CC BY for everybody else, and buying it does not take them down.
  • Not corrected for you. The mandate in it is the starting mandate, written to be corrected, and correcting it is yours to do.
