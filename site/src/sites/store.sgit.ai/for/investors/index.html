You are backing a company, and want somebody to look

An investor who needs a review of a company they are backing, and needs it to be somebody's work rather than a scan.

You arrive asking: “What would somebody who knows what they are doing find?”

What the market looks like here

No productised, affordable, signed investor review exists at any price — and every component of one is already written. It is the emptiest quadrant on this site, and the two upper tiers point straight at it.

What you are actually buying

A person's time, and in tier 4 a team's. A time confirmed, what they read before the call, and what you get afterwards in writing.

The offers you are shown

  • t3 — Tier 3 — £500 — Can you correct it for my situation? — One price means one standing payment link, printable on a card.
  • t4 — Tier 4 — £1,500 — Can somebody go through it with my team? — One price means one standing payment link, printable on a card.
  • add-opinion — Add-on — By depth band — Will somebody put their name to it? — There is no code behind this one.
  • add-formats — Add-on — By depth band — Can I have the same result in a form the board will read? — This attaches to another offer and is priced against its depth band, so it has no checkout of its own.

What you are not buying

  • A signature. A reviewed opinion issued by the company and signed by a named professional is a separate add-on, its wording does not exist, and buying an hour of review does not buy one. {{claim:opinion-wording-absent}}
  • Raw findings. Every finding that reaches you has been reproduced first, because recall-optimised agents run at 0.388 precision. What is sold is triage. {{claim:precision-0388}}
  • A mark of conformity, an accreditation, or an audit opinion. None of those describes this, and that language appears on no deliverable here.
