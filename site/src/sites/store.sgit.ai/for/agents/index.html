You run agents today

A team with an agent already in production, holding the credential it runs on.

You arrive asking: “What did I actually grant it?”

What the market looks like here

The market anchors between eight and one hundred and fifty thousand dollars, and nothing sits underneath it. That gap is the whole opportunity: somebody who wants to know what they granted an agent has a choice today between a five-figure engagement and nothing.

What you are actually buying

The delta: the union of what a credential permits, against what its holder is authorised and expected to do, and the excess authority between the two. It is computed when you open it and never stored. It is not a thing we keep about you.

The offers you are shown

  • t1 — Tier 1 — £10 — Can I just have the pack? — https://buy.stripe.com/dRmbITfDa7E31Z8fY15os03 ↗
  • t2 — Tier 2 — £50 — Can I have a vault I hold the keys to? — One price means one standing payment link, printable on a card.
  • add-formats — Add-on — By depth band — Can I have the same result in a form the board will read? — This attaches to another offer and is priced against its depth band, so it has no checkout of its own.

What you are not buying

  • A judgement about whether you meet anything. Tier 2 selects from a published regulation; it does not grade you against it.
  • A promise that the measurement ran. Tier 1's application was built in August and has not been located, and until it is, what arrives is your answers and the questions. {{claim:t1-app-not-located}}
