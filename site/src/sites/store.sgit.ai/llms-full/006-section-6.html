How to book

Buy the level, and the sessions are scheduled after. Your order↗ takes you to the payment provider; the conversation follows the purchase rather than gating it, which is the opposite of how consulting is usually sold and is the reason it can be bought from a card at all.

At the event: the code on the card, which redirects to the delivery page for that level and says what it covers before you commit.

There is no form on this site — it does not collect anything, from anybody, ever.
