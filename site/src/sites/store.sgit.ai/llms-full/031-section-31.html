The same question, from three sides

The three buyers on this site arrive at the same map from different directions, and the configurator changes its frame accordingly. Who each one is for↗.

Arrives askingWhat the map is drawn for
You are a startup↗What does our estate look like to somebody who is about to ask?Map it upwards. Diligence is coming, the questions are the same every time, and the difference is whether the answers already exist in writing
You are an investor↗What is the agent in this company actually doing, and what was it allowed to do?Map both sides of a company you do not operate: the permitted half from its credentials, the expected half from its own account
You run agents today↗What did we actually grant the things we are already running?Map your own estate. The credentials were issued over months by different people, and no document says what their union permits
