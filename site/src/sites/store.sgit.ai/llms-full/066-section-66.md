## What the states mean

**Four of the ten states are ways of saying "not yet"**, and that proportion is the
point rather than an embarrassment.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **exists and runs** | Built, and it runs today |
| **measured** | Measured by our own pipeline, on a named workload and date |
| **read, not run** | Read from a published source on this date. Never executed by us |
| **projected** | Arithmetic, not an invoice. The workings are shown |
| **specified, not built** | A specification. The thing it specifies does not exist |
| **delivered by a person** | A named security professional does this work and signs it off. It is somebody's time rather than a pipeline, which is why its delivery estimate depends on a calendar |
| **built, not located** | It was built. It has not been found since, and nothing here promises it until it is |
| **a booking, not a download** | What is bought is a person's time, not a file |
| **part exists** | One half runs. The half carrying the guarantee does not |
| **does not exist yet** | It does not exist. It is listed so nobody proposes it as new |
