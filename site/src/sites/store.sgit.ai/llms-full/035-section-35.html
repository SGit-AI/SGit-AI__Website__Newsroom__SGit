What happens to it

Nothing, unless you send it. A brief is written to your browser's own local storage and reaches nothing else. There is **no form, input, textarea or select anywhere in this site's output, and **no page that sells anything opens a network connection — both are build checks rather than intentions — so exporting a brief hands the file to you. {{claim:lab-brief-stays-local}}

What the deliverables look like is a separate question, and it is not answered here. This area is the purchasing half. Examples of what comes out the other end belong on their own pages and do not exist yet.
