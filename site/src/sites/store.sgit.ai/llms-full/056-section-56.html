Paying, in three lines

You build an order in your own browser and pay on the provider's own page. No account, no cookie, and no form, input or field anywhere on this site — a build check holds that line, so filtering is buttons, quantity is buttons, and your card is somebody else's business. What reaches the payment provider is the amount and an order reference carrying the codes for what you bought.
