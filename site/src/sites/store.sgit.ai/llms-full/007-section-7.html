The rule

The store owns e-commerce. riskmandate.ai owns the material.

Everything that is a cart, a workflow, a redirection or a payment happens on store.sgit.ai. Everything that is a product, a list, an Agent Behaviour Policy, an explanation or a screenshot belongs to riskmandate.ai. Every other question about where something lives follows from that sentence, including which side a file that is currently on the wrong one has to move to.
