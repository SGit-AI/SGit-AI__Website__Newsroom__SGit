## Why this page exists at all

Because the alternative is that one of these eight gets proposed in three weeks as
a fresh idea, and the reasoning that put it here has to be rebuilt from nothing.

And because a store's real risk is not that it sells too little — it is that the
four things it does sell quietly grow to cover the eight it does not. Every one of
[the four delivery pages](/offers/) says **what does not arrive**, and this page is
the same discipline pointed one level up.
