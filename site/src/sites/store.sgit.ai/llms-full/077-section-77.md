## The free thing, and the paid thing

**The templates are free and public**, with published read keys, at
riskmandate.ai. Go and read one before you buy anything here. That is not a
generosity and it is not a funnel: a policy nobody can check is a policy asking to
be trusted, and the whole point of this document type is that it can be checked.

**What is priced here is an instance.** The same template with the mandate
corrected, your name on the licence and no public key on it — and at the upper two
levels, the correction done with you rather than by you. **The paid thing is not
access and it is not customisation. It is independence:** at every level more of
the result is yours and less of it depends on us still being here.
