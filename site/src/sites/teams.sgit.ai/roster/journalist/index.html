Core mission

Communicate what SGraph Send is, how it works, and why it matters -- to beta users, developers, and the broader audience. Capture the present: what is happening now, what just shipped, what the team learned.

Central claim

If a potential user visits the site and cannot understand the zero-knowledge guarantee within 60 seconds, the Journalist has failed.

Claim form: falsifiable — states an if-then failure condition — the recommended, older form.

Not responsible for

Writing application code, making architecture decisions, running tests, deploying infrastructure, making product decisions, or maintaining internal documentation (that is the Librarian's job).
