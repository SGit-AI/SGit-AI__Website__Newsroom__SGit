graphs.sgit.ai — Brief Pack

Start with 00__BRIEF.md.

A brief pack for building graphs.sgit.ai, a new sibling site on the sgit.ai network focused on graphs — specifically on meaning through connectivity.

Prepared 21 August 2026 against the-cyber-boardroom/SGraph-AI__App__Send @ v0.33.62, with sgit.ai, pki.sgit.ai, nhi.sgit.ai and sg-sentinel.sgit.ai surveyed for house conventions.

Tiers used in the manifest

  • Tier 0 (19) — publishable near-as-is, highest value
  • Tier 1 (57) — publishable with framing, a rewrite, or a render
  • Tier 2 (9) — needs de-scoping, a "not adopted" label, or a future-vision banner
  • Tier 3 (4) — do not publish (real personal data, third-party legal read, commercial material)

Verification

Every repo path in this pack — including all 89 manifest rows — was checked to exist at v0.33.62. Two filenames were corrected against the repo during preparation.

Numbers for the three already-published graph vaults come from the live pages on sgit.ai, not from the repo. Numbers for repo artefacts come from parsing the documents. The pack never mixes the two.

Licence

All content in this pack is released under CC BY 4.0. See LICENSE.md.

Per the decision of 21 August 2026, the same applies to the corpus and to the content of every *.sgit.ai site — see 09__licensing-decision.md. Gap G8 in 08__gaps-and-fresh-writing.md is closed by that decision; what remains is mechanical.

The one-line summary

The material exists — ~55 conceptual documents (~135,000 words), 20 worked graph applications, 13 Wardley maps, three live graph vaults. The gap is routing, rendering, and one essay that was never written.
