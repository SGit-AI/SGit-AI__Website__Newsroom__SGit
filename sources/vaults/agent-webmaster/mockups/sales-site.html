How it works

Four steps, and the fourth one is the point.

1A callThirty to sixty minutes. What you do, who the site is for, what you hate about the current one. Send us your logo, photos and text however is easy.
2A preview in daysYou look at it on your phone and tell us what is wrong. Two or three rounds is normal. Then it goes live on your domain.
3You own itThe site is plain files in a repository under your name, with every change recorded. If you ever leave us, you take everything.
4You change it by askingYour own chat with the webmaster agent. Hours, prices, photos, notices: live in a minute. Bigger changes go to a preview and we check them.
