# 10. Open questions

1. **Where does the memo best happen?** At the end, or also in the middle, while the player collects balls?
2. **How long should the player's view be?** Three points for the next games worked in the demo; real players will say.
3. **Who pays first: players, coaches or clubs?**
4. **How often are lanes folded into the record,** and who triggers it: the coach on submit, the player on open, or a schedule?
5. **How do players give and take back coach access** without handling raw keys?
6. **What does a club need to see** to say yes, without holding the players' records?
7. **What is the smallest useful clip?** Ten seconds of one point, or whole games?
