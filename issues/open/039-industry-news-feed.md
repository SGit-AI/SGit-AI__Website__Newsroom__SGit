# A daily industry news feed on the vaults' topics, as JSON the newsroom consumes

Topics: EU AI Act, OWASP, risk and GRC, security incidents, and the topics of the published vaults. An
agent (using ChatGPT, Perplexity or another LLM with browsing) writes `feeds/YYYY-MM-DD.json` (items with
url, title, summary, topic, why it matters to which project); the Librarian files them; the Journalist gets
an "In the world" section. Start here; move to its own site later.
